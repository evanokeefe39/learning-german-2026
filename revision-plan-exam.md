# A1 German Exam Revision Plan

**Exam:** Thursday March 26, 2026
**Format:** Reading-only lexical understanding (vocabulary recognition, fill blanks, choose correct grammar forms)
**Scope:** All 12 chapters — Spektrum Deutsch A1
**Key insight:** This is a recognition exam. You need to recognize correct German when you see it, not produce it from scratch.

---

## How This Plan Works

- Move through Phases A-G in order
- Every phase has a MAX TIME — if you hit it, MOVE ON even if you didn't finish
- Mark what you didn't finish — it becomes priority input for Iteration 2
- A fast incomplete pass is more valuable than a slow perfect one
- Aim to complete Phases A-F as Iteration 1 today (~4.5 hours)
- Iteration 2+ goes deeper on weak spots only

**Total estimated time for Iteration 1: ~4.5 hours**
